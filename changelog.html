Change log

What changed in each public version of the OPTCG Judge Trainer.

Version 1.6 · 2026-10-25

  • 39 new tournament penalty and infraction questions: repeat-offence upgrades, Suspensions, how penalties are recorded and reported, and the manual's own infraction examples.
  • Clearer wording and evidence on 13 existing rules and policy questions after an independent review. Saved progress on those questions carries over.
  • Version number and this change log.

Version 1.5 · 2026-10-25

  • Questions held back for conflicting official sources stay excluded even when copied, imported or re-added.
  • Deck Practice attack/Blocker and Enel Trigger questions now state every condition they depend on.
  • Rule 8-2-2 explanation names its supporting FAQ.

Version 1.4 · 2026-10-21

  • Alvida (EB05-021) card text is now labeled official.

Version 1.3 · 2026-10-21

  • Deck Practice and VS tabs (experimental): rulings questions on a deck's cards, and card-versus-card scenarios.
  • Card ability rows in the card library.
  • 1,141 practice questions.

Version 1.2 · 2026-10-21

  • Classic and Modern question sets. Modern questions quote the official card text.
  • Clearer wording on six questions.

Version 1.1 · 2026-10-20

  • 1,097 practice questions with clearer options.
  • Estimated difficulty labels.

Version 1.0 · 2026-10-19

  • First public version: topic practice, missed-question review, timed mock exams, the official Q&A drill and the card library.
